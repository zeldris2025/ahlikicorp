About Us

More than a business.
A Samoan supply chain.

Ah Liki Investment Corporation Limited is a Samoan-owned, multi-ventured company built over more than two decades. What began in the fields and markets of Samoa now moves goods from the wharf to the shelf, and from our own farms to family tables, across the whole country.

Our roots

Built from the ground up

Ah Liki began the way many Samoan businesses do — with hard work on the land and a determination to serve the community around it. That beginning still shapes how the group operates: close to the customer, careful with quality, and invested in Samoa rather than extracting from it.

Over two decades that single enterprise grew into five companies spanning wholesale distribution, supermarket retail, brewing, restaurants and agriculture — each run to the same standard, and each strengthening the others.

What we do

From the wharf to your shelf

We run one of the largest supply-chain networks in Samoa. Containers land at the port, move through our warehousing, and go out on our own fleet to retailers, hotels, restaurants, schools and government customers nationwide.

Owning the chain end to end is what lets us hold prices steady, keep stock available and get goods to Savai’i and rural Upolu as reliably as to Apia. It is also why our supermarkets, restaurants and brewery can depend on supply that others cannot.
